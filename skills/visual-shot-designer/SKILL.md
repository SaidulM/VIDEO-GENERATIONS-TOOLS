# Cinematic Visual Shot Designer

For each scene describe:
1. Subject and environment.
2. Shot size and camera angle.
3. Camera movement (slow push-in, pan, tilt, tracking, locked shot).
4. Lighting and atmosphere.
5. A visual transition to the next scene.

Avoid repeating the same composition. Use a coherent palette. For Islamic content, favour symbolic environments and non-figurative visuals. Keep prompts concise and suitable for a text-to-video or image-to-video model.
